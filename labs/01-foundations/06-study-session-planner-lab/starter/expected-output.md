```text
Dotnet Academy: Study Session Planner Lab
-----------------------------------------
Learner: Ada
Weekly topic target: 5
Completed topics: 3
Practice hours logged: 7.5
Average hours per completed topic: 2.5
Mentor: Unassigned
Recent warning: None
Readiness: 2 topics left before the weekly target is complete.
Next actions:
1. Review one CLI command you have already used successfully.
2. Run the console app again with your learner name.
3. Finish one more focused Stage 1 topic practice block.
Debug tip: Re-run after each small change and confirm the parsed values still look correct.
```

The important part is that the output comes from typed values, helper methods, conditional logic, and at least one safe fallback for missing data.
