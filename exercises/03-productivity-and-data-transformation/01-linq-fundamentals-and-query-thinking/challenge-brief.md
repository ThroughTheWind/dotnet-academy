# Challenge Brief

Build a console app named `StudyQueryReportConsole` that turns a small in-memory study log into a readable report.

Your app must:

- filter and order a completed-items section with LINQ
- build a focus list for items that still need attention
- group the data into a short category summary
- explain, in comments or notes, what question each query is answering

Keep the queries small enough that another learner could read them from top to bottom.
