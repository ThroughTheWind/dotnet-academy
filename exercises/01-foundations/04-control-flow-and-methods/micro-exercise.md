# 04 Control Flow And Methods Micro Exercise

## Prompt

Write a method named `GetStudyLabel(int completedTopics)` that returns one of two strings based on whether the learner completed at least 3 topics.

Then call the method and use a `for` loop to print `Practice session 1` and `Practice session 2`.

## Constraints

- Keep the method focused on one decision.
- Use exactly one `if` and `else` branch inside the method.
- Keep the loop to two iterations.

## Verification

You are done when the app prints the label returned by the method and the two repeated session lines.
